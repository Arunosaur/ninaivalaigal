# Ninaivalaigal Trademark Usage Guidelines

**Version 1.0 | Effective Date: October 2025**

This document outlines the proper use of Medhasys LLC's trademarks, including "Ninaivalaigal," "Medhasys," and related marks.

## Our Trademarks

The following are trademarks or service marks of Medhasys LLC ("Medhasys"):

- **Ninaivalaigal™** - The software platform name
- **Medhasys™** - The company name
- **e^M** - The memory intelligence notation
- Associated logos, design marks, and product names

These marks are referred to collectively as the "Marks."

## General Principles

✅ **You MAY**:
- Use the Marks to refer to Ninaivalaigal software accurately in documentation
- State that your software is "compatible with Ninaivalaigal" or "works with Ninaivalaigal"
- Use the Marks in blog posts, articles, or presentations about the software
- Display the Marks on your website when linking to our official resources

❌ **You MAY NOT**:
- Use the Marks in a way that suggests Medhasys endorses your product/service
- Use the Marks as part of your company name, product name, or domain name
- Modify, combine, or abbreviate the Marks (e.g., don't use "Nina" alone)
- Register domain names, social media handles, or app names containing the Marks
- Use the Marks in a misleading or disparaging manner

## Permitted Uses (No Permission Required)

### 1. Editorial and Informational Use
You may use the Marks when writing about Ninaivalaigal, provided you:
- Use the full mark ("Ninaivalaigal," not "Nina")
- Include the ™ symbol on first prominent use
- Include this disclaimer: "Ninaivalaigal is a trademark of Medhasys LLC"

Example:
> "This tutorial shows how to integrate with Ninaivalaigal™ for memory management."

### 2. Compatible Products and Services
If you offer a product/service compatible with Ninaivalaigal, you may state:
- "Works with Ninaivalaigal"
- "Compatible with Ninaivalaigal"
- "Designed for Ninaivalaigal"

You must NOT imply:
- Official endorsement or partnership
- That your product is Ninaivalaigal itself
- That Medhasys is responsible for your product

### 3. Community Websites and Resources
You may use the Marks for community resources like:
- "Ninaivalaigal Users Forum"
- "Ninaivalaigal Tutorial Blog"
- "Learn Ninaivalaigal Workshop"

Provided you:
- Clearly state "Not affiliated with Medhasys LLC"
- Do not use official Medhasys branding or logos
- Do not monetize using the Marks without permission

## Restricted Uses (Permission Required)

Contact trademark@medhasys.com for permission to:

1. **Use Marks in product/company names**
   - Example: "NinaivaBot" requires permission

2. **Create derivative marks**
   - Example: "Powered by Ninaivalaigal" badges

3. **Commercial training or consulting**
   - Example: "Certified Ninaivalaigal Consultant"

4. **Merchandise or swag**
   - Example: T-shirts, stickers with Marks

5. **Events and conferences**
   - Example: "NinaivalaigalCon 2025"

## Prohibited Uses

**Never** use the Marks to:
- Suggest false affiliation with Medhasys
- Disparage Ninaivalaigal or Medhasys
- Violate any laws or regulations
- Promote illegal, harmful, or offensive content
- Compete directly with Medhasys products

## Domain Names and Social Media

❌ **Do NOT** register:
- Domain names containing "Ninaivalaigal" or "Medhasys"
- Social media handles like @Ninaivalaigal or @MedhasysOfficial
- App store names including the Marks

Exception: We may grant permission for official community domains (e.g., ninaivalaigal-users.org) on a case-by-case basis.

## Logo Usage

The Ninaivalaigal and Medhasys logos are **not** open source and require explicit permission for any use.

**Logos are NOT covered** under the MIT, Apache 2.0, or Elastic licenses. They remain proprietary to Medhasys LLC.

## Proper Attribution

When using the Marks, include this notice:
```
Ninaivalaigal is a trademark of Medhasys LLC.
```

For prominent use (like a book cover or conference), use:
```
Ninaivalaigal™ and Medhasys™ are trademarks of Medhasys LLC.
Use of these marks does not imply endorsement.
```

## Open Source License vs. Trademark

**Important Distinction**:
- The **software code** is licensed under various open source licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, etc.)
- The **trademarks** are NOT open source and remain owned by Medhasys LLC

You can fork and modify the MIT-licensed code freely, but you **cannot** call your fork "Ninaivalaigal" without permission.
